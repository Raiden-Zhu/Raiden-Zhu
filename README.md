## Hi, I'm Tongtian Zhu

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Computer Science Department of [Zhejiang University (ZJU)](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/), supervised by Professors [Can Wang](https://person.zju.edu.cn/en/wangcan) and [Chun Chen](https://person.zju.edu.cn/en/0082004).

### Research interests
My research focuses on understanding learning mechanisms and developing methods grounded in these insights. I am particularly interested in:

- **Language model training** — optimizers, looped architectures, and efficient parallelism.
- **Decentralized learning** — [training dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02913), [generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.12680), [data influence](https://openreview.net/forum?id=2TIYkqieKw), and [communication allocation](https://openreview.net/forum?id=lWGMbJRCtQ).
- **Foundations of deep learning** — [learning dynamics / implicit bias](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02913), [symmetries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09189), and [emergence of mergeability](https://openreview.net/forum?id=lWGMbJRCtQ).

Feel free to [email me](mailto:raiden@zju.edu.cn) to discuss research or collaboration.
